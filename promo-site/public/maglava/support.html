Rise or burn. Swing through 40 magnetic climbs above the lava.

How to play

Tap a color button to launch toward a matching node. Letters identify the colors and outlines show available targets. You can retarget in flight. Cross checkpoints and reach the gold-ringed exit before the lava catches you.

On Mac, click the onscreen controls or use R/B/Y/G. W/S/A/D also select red, blue, yellow and green. Escape pauses.

Pause, retry and settings

The pause button stops the run. Resume continues from the same position; Retry Stage restarts that stage. After a death, the game reconnects at your most recent checkpoint. Sound, haptics and reduced motion are available in Settings & how to play. Completed progress is saved on this device. If the operating system closes the app, a live run restarts from stage selection.
